Check timings:

Once a day, CTIT runs every clang-tidy check on every test project with -enable-check-profile and records two times. Check time is the time spent inside the checks, summed over all files and parallel jobs. Analysis time is the wall-clock time of run-clang-tidy on a project, including parsing.

Show:
Written like clang-tidy -checks, e.g. modernize-*,-modernize-use-auto. Text without * matches the check with exactly that name, or else every check that contains it. Sum draws the matching checks as one line; compare draws one line per check.

Documentation

Hover over a day to see its values, and click it to see the LLVM changes since the previous run. Drag to zoom in and double-click to zoom out. A gray vertical line marks a runner change: the times on either side come from different hardware and cannot be compared.

Per project:

Checks:

Each matching check's time in the latest run, summed over the selected projects, and its change since the previous run and since the run a week earlier. Changes of at least 10% and 1 s are colored. Click a check to show only that check, or click a column header to sort.
